# Aidan Gildea

High school junior in Los Angeles. I like robotics, reinforcement learning, and building from the bottom of the stack up: instruction sets, firmware, control loops, and simulation.

**Current positions**
- Lead programmer on VEX AI Robotics Team **10700W**, working on sim-to-real reinforcement learning and computer vision: train a policy in simulation, then run it on the real robot.
- Research intern at **UCLA SRILAB**, building a 3D reconstruction pipeline (Gaussian splatting + photogrammetry) that turns Aria Gen 2 captures into simulation-ready Isaac Sim scenes.

**Interests:** reinforcement learning, sim-to-real robotics, 3D reconstruction, computer architecture, rocketry.

**A few projects**
- [ContactSplat](https://github.com/Aidan-Gildea/ContactSplat): egocentric capture to Isaac Sim environment with aligned collision geometry and Gaussian splats.
- [full-stack-8bit-cpu](https://github.com/Aidan-Gildea/full-stack-8bit-cpu): a gate-level 8-bit CPU, its ALU rebuilt on a breadboard, plus the ISA, assembler, and emulator that program it.
- [robot-arm-3dof](https://github.com/Aidan-Gildea/robot-arm-3dof): a 3-DOF arm for about $100, with inverse kinematics derived from first principles.
- [RobotCarRL](https://github.com/Aidan-Gildea/RobotCarRL): an RL training pipeline and a custom Arduino neural network runtime that teach a two-wheeled robot to balance.
